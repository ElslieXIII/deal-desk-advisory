What it was built for

Every mandate, investor and conversation in one place

A corporate finance advisory team runs many mandates at once. Each needs the right investors approached, followed up and tracked to a decision, while the team keeps hundreds of relationships warm and looks for the next mandate. That usually means distribution lists in spreadsheets, notes in inboxes and nobody quite sure who last spoke to whom.

Deal Desk · Advisory keeps mandates, investor distribution, the CRM and relationships in one shared desk. It answers the everyday questions: where is each deal, which investors have seen it and what did they say, who should we approach next, and who have we not spoken to in months.

What it does

  • Runs mandates from first idea to close: pipeline, live deals by stage and probability, fees, and next steps with owners and due dates.
  • Builds investor distribution lists per deal, tracks each investor's status and feedback, and exports to Excel.
  • Finds people you haven't spoken to in one, three, six or twelve months and books the catch-ups.
  • Shortlists investors for a deal from a plain-English request, with the reasons and gaps for each.
  • Spots companies likely to need an adviser, from triggers such as a PE owner four years in, a new CFO or debt coming due, and drafts a pitch angle and pitch note.
  • Logs every change with who made it, and supports undo and redo.

How it's built

One desk, shared by the whole team

Shared and accountable

Meeting briefings from notes, emails, deals and news; plain-English search for investors; pitch angles and pitch notes on companies to approach; CRM updates suggested after each note.
